Why Data is Different

One mistake can nuke your entire database.

Vibe-coding apps is forgiving — a bad push ships a broken UI, you fix it and redeploy. The blast radius is bounded.

Vibe-coding data is not forgiving. It's very easy for an agent to inadvertently run:

DELETE FROM orders

No WHERE clause. No warning. Records gone — potentially millions of them, across tables that may have no backup. By the time you notice, the damage has already propagated downstream.

This isn't hypothetical — it's already happening in production. Prelight gives agents a sandbox that is correct by construction — so they can iterate freely without the risk of an irrecoverable mistake.
